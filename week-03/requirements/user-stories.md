# User stories — Smart Campus study room booking

### US-01
**Story:** As a Student, I want to view available study rooms for a specific date and time slot, so that I can find an open room that fits my study schedule.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** Room availability updates in real time to reflect current bookings and administrative blocks.

### US-02
**Story:** As a Student, I want to book an available study room for up to two hours in the future, so that I can reserve a quiet space for individual or group study.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** A student can submit a valid booking only if the chosen slot does not overlap with an existing reservation or block.

### US-03
**Story:** As a Student, I want to cancel my active room booking, so that the room becomes available for other students if my plans change.
**Priority:** Medium
**Assumption:** Students can only cancel their own future active bookings.

### US-04
**Story:** As a Student, I want to receive an automated confirmation when I book or cancel a room, so that I have proof of my reservation status.
**Priority:** Medium
**Assumption:** Confirmations are generated automatically by the system immediately following a successful booking or cancellation.

### US-05
**Story:** As an Administrator, I want to block or unblock study rooms for servicing, so that students cannot book rooms that are temporarily unavailable.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** Blocking a room takes immediate effect and prevents any student booking during the specified period.

### US-06
**Story:** As an Administrator, I want to review room usage statistics, so that I can analyze room utilization trends and optimize campus space management.
**Priority:** Low
**Assumption:** Usage reports aggregate historical booking data without modifying active reservations.
